# Task 001: Classify every unclassified census clause

## Objective

Give all 408 census clauses a family or an explicit recorded disposition, so
acceptance gate 1 -- "all candidate clauses have a recorded disposition" -- can
be evaluated at all.

`effect_semantics.py` emits every segmented clause and marks the ones its
matchers do not recognise `pending_classification`. There are 82 of them, and
nothing downstream can be estimated, sequenced or deferred while they have no
family: they are not known to be work, and not known to be ignorable.

This card classifies them deterministically. It does not structure them. A
clause that gains the family `token_create` here is still unstructured
afterwards, and that is the correct outcome -- the point is to convert an
unknown into a scoped known.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] No census row is left with an empty `families` list and no recorded disposition; `clauses_pending_classification` in the census summary reaches zero
- [ ] Bare keyword clauses (47 of the 82: `Trample`, `Flying`, `Flash`, `Vigilance`, `Reach`, `deathtouch`, and comma-joined combinations) are recognised as static keyword grants and disposed of with a stated reason, not silently dropped
- [ ] Mode headers (9 of the 82, e.g. `Choose one -`) are recognised as structural markers rather than effects, and disposed of with a stated reason
- [ ] Named-mechanic clauses (10 of the 82, e.g. `Storied (...)`, `Kicker {2}{W}{W} (...)`) are attributed to the mechanic that owns them, and the disposition names the layer that represents them
- [ ] The 16 remaining clauses map to families that already exist in the vocabulary, with no new family invented: 6 land-ETB replacement, 2 `Ward {N}`, 2 `Enchant creature` attachment, 2 additional-trigger copy, 2 zone-change return/move, 1 granted-abilities, 1 characteristic-defining power
- [ ] Classification is deterministic and contains no card-name or UUID branches (repair-instructions acceptance gate 7); the same Oracle text classifies the same way on every run
- [ ] A test asserts zero rows carry an empty `families` list without a disposition, and fails if a future clause regresses to unclassified
- [ ] A test asserts each of the four disposition kinds the spec defines is reachable, so a disposition that no rule can produce is caught
- [ ] Two serial census generations are byte-identical
- [ ] Frozen artifacts are unchanged, and `effect_records.jsonl` is unchanged -- this card classifies, it does not structure

## Requirements

- The four dispositions are the ones the repair instructions define: structured and projected; structured but intentionally not pair-projected, with a reason; deliberately ignored, with a reason; unresolved or nonexecutable, with a reason and provenance. Do not invent a fifth
- A keyword ability is not an effect clause, but it is also not nothing. Dispose of it with a reason that names where keywords are represented, rather than deleting the row
- Do not add a family that no clause needs. The 16 residue all map to existing families; if one genuinely does not, report that rather than widening the vocabulary silently
- Do not touch `effect_records.jsonl` or any frozen artifact. A classification card that changes structured output has done something else as well
- Keyword detection works from a declared keyword set, not from a per-card list

## Notes

The 82 were measured on 2026-09-02 and bucket as follows:

    47  bare keyword abilities
    16  genuine effects the matchers miss
    10  named mechanic plus reminder parenthetical
     9  mode headers

The 16 in full, with the family each belongs to:

    replacement   This land enters tapped.                    (Lake-town, Goblin-town,
                                                               Iron Hills, Elvenking's
                                                               Halls, Mirkwood)
    replacement   This land enters tapped unless you control an Equipment.
                                                              (The Lonely Mountain)
    keyword       Ward {3} / Ward {2}                         (Gandalf, Lake-town Mariners)
    attachment    Enchant creature                            (Eagle's Rescue,
                                                               Enchanted River's Grotto)
    copy          ...that ability triggers an additional time. (Wizard's Staff, Bifur)
    return_move   Put a card exiled with this Saga into its owner's hand.
                                                              (Roads Go Ever, Ever On)
    return_move   Target creature's owner puts it on their choice of the top or
                  bottom of their library.                    (Uneasy Partings)
    grant_ability Thranduil has all activated abilities of all Elf cards in your
                  graveyard.                                  (Thranduil)
    modify_pt     Esgaroth Garrison's power is equal to the number of creatures
                  you control.                                (Esgaroth Garrison)

Note that `Ward {N}` failed keyword detection only because of its reminder
parenthetical, and `Enchant creature` is an attachment clause whose family is
already carried by the equip tier. Neither is a new capability; both are gaps in
the matcher's reach.

The census emitting zero-family rows at all is a review-driven decision from
Phase 2 pt2 -- the comment at `effect_semantics.py` line 155 records that an
undetected clause must still appear, precisely so it can be counted. This card
is the other half of that decision: counted, then disposed.
